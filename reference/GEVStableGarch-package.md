# GEVStableGarch: ARMA-GARCH/APARCH models with stable, GEV and GAt innovations

Simulation, estimation and forecasting of ARMA-GARCH/APARCH models whose
innovations follow a stable (S0), GEV or GAt distribution, or any user
defined innovation distribution.
